THE THREE PILLARS

Why Discerning Families Choose GreenFarms.

In an era of industrial shortcuts and adulterated cooking oils, we preserve the uncompromising gold standard of authentic Desi Ghee.

01

100% Cultured Butter Base

Commercial brands separate cream with industrial centrifuges and boil it directly at scorching heat. GreenFarms strictly begins with slow-churned cultured village butter (makkhan), yielding natural lactic enzymes and unmistakable golden aroma.

Zero Industrial Cream
02

Granular Danedar Texture

True Desi Ghee is neither greasy oil nor smooth wax. Our slow-simmering process in heavy brass cauldrons allows the natural milkfat triglycerides to crystallize into exquisite, melt-in-the-mouth danedar grains.

Natural Crystal Formation
