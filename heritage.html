THE CHRONICLE OF PURITY

Two Decades of Refusal & Devotion

How a smallholding farm initiative in rural Punjab grew into Pakistan's benchmark for authentic butter-churned Pure Desi Ghee.

2006
GENESIS IN PUNJAB

The Foundation of GreenFarms.

Founded as a dedicated family enterprise in the fertile dairy heartlands of Punjab. Witnessing commercial processors shift to boiling raw dairy cream using industrial centrifuges, the founders established GreenFarms with one mandate: return to pure cultured butter (makkhan). Initial batches were churned by hand and distributed strictly to close family and community patrons.

First Hand-Simmered Batches
2012
FAIR DAIRY ALLIANCES

Direct Village Smallholder Partnerships

To maintain uncompromised raw milk standards without middlemen adulteration, GreenFarms established direct fair-wage partnerships with traditional dairy smallholders across Punjab villages. Cows and buffaloes graze naturally on green seasonal forage, ensuring high natural beta-carotene content and vibrant golden butterfat.

Ethical Grazing • Fair Wages
2018
SCALING INTEGRITY

Rejecting Industrial Shortcuts

As nationwide demand surged, commercial consultants recommended high-heat steam distillation and centrifugal cream separation to cut clarification time from 4 hours down to 20 minutes. GreenFarms firmly rejected these shortcuts, investing instead in specialized small-batch brass cauldrons to preserve the delicate nutty flavor and crystalline danedar granulations.

Zero Chemicals • Low-Flame Clarification
2026

AUTHENTIC VISUAL ARCHIVES

The Craft That Never Changed

A glimpse into the unchanged village traditions that yield our golden crystalline ghee.

HERITAGE STEP 01

Free-Range Forage

Our partner herds graze freely on natural Punjab grasslands, producing naturally nutrient-dense milk rich in fat-soluble vitamins.

HERITAGE STEP 02

Bilona Churning

Cultured whole milk is churned bi-directionally to separate light, aromatic butterfat (makkhan) from nourishing buttermilk.

HERITAGE STEP 03

Slow Simmering

Low-flame simmering in heavy brass cauldrons gently evaporates moisture and caramelizes milk solids into sweet golden crystals.

HERITAGE STEP 04

Airtight Preservation

Poured warm into 500g premium food-grade plastic jars, creating a protective airtight seal that maintains peak freshness for 12 months.
