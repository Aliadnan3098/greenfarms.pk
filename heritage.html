CHRONICLE OF PURITY

The Two-Decade Journey

From a humble family kitchen in rural Punjab to Pakistan's benchmark for genuine butter-churned Pure Desi Ghee in 500g premium plastic jars.

2006
MILESTONE 01 • THE BEGINNING

The Kitchen Counter Rebellion

Founded as a dedicated family initiative in Punjab in response to urban markets being flooded with cream-boiled and adulterated ghee. Recognizing that commercial brands had quietly abandoned authentic churning, GreenFarms began sourcing pure village butter from trusted smallholder dairy farmers to craft small-batch ghee strictly for family, neighbors, and word-of-mouth patrons.

2012
MILESTONE 02 • DIRECT COLLECTIVES

Direct Village Partnerships

As demand spread throughout Lahore and Islamabad, GreenFarms built direct relationships with rural dairy collectives in the Punjab heartland, bypassing exploitative middlemen. By paying premium rates for grass-fed milk and authentic white butter, we guaranteed ethical cattle fodder, honest living wages for village families, and uncompromising churning standards.

2018
MILESTONE 03 • INTEGRITY AT SCALE

Scaling Without Compromise

To meet growing demand from culinary connoisseurs, we expanded our clarification capacity while strictly maintaining our principles: zero industrial cream centrifuges, zero chemical bleaching, zero artificial flavor essences, and no high-pressure boiling. Every batch continued to simmer slowly in heavy vessels over low flame.

2026
MILESTONE 04 • TWENTY-YEAR LEGACY

The Uncompromised Gold Standard

Today, GreenFarms delivers lab-verified, butter-churned Pure Desi Ghee in 500g premium shatterproof plastic jars directly to homes and culinary professionals across Pakistan. With independent laboratory verification on every batch (99.8% milkfat, zero palm oil or vanaspati adulteration), we honor the exact artisanal process we set out with in 2006.
