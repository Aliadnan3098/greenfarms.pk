COMMON QUERIES

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear, transparent answers about our butter-churned ghee and 500g jar packaging.

We use premium, certified food-grade, BPA-free plastic jars specifically to protect your order during shipping. Glass jars frequently suffer catastrophic breakage and oil leaks during courier transit across Pakistan's highways. Our 500g plastic jars are completely shatterproof and feature a tamper-evident airtight induction foil seal beneath the black threaded lid, preserving fresh butter aromatics and zero contamination.

True butter ghee naturally sets into granular, crystalline danedar crystals when cooled at room temperature. Cream-boiled ghee remains a flat, heavy paste that feels oily and acid-inducing. Furthermore, GreenFarms ghee is independently certified with a Reichert-Meissl (RM) value of 28.6+, confirming authentic volatile fatty acids characteristic strictly of churned butterfat.

Store your 500g jar in a cool, dry kitchen pantry away from direct sunlight. Always use a dry, clean spoon. Do not refrigerate the jar, as cold moisture can condense inside and disrupt the natural granular crystal matrix. Stored properly at room temperature, GreenFarms Pure Desi Ghee has a stable shelf life of 12 months.

We ship nationwide across Pakistan via premier express courier partners. Delivery typically takes 2 to 3 business days for major cities (Lahore, Karachi, Islamabad, Rawalpindi, Faisalabad, Multan, Peshawar) and 3 to 4 business days for outlying regions. Shipping is completely FREE on orders of PKR 3,000 or more (2 or more 500g jars). Single-jar orders incur a flat PKR 250 shipping fee.

We stand 100% behind the integrity of our ghee. If any GreenFarms jar fails accredited third-party food laboratory purity testing for genuine butterfat, we will refund your full order price immediately and reimburse 100% of your laboratory testing fees.
