Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about butter-churned Pure Desi Ghee, our 500g premium plastic jars, testing, and delivery.

Our custom 500g food-grade plastic jars are specifically selected for safety, convenience, and optimal preservation. They are 100% BPA-free, completely shatterproof during courier transit across Pakistan, lightweight for daily kitchen handling with greasy hands, and fitted with an airtight inner seal that locks in the aromatic volatile esters of fresh butter-churned ghee.
Most commercial brands use industrial centrifuges to separate raw cream and boil it directly at high temperatures without culturing. GreenFarms strictly cultures whole milk with natural dahi cultures, slow-churns it into white village butter (makkhan), and simmers it slowly in heavy brass vessels. This fermentative step creates rich, complex caramelized aroma compounds (lactones and diacetyl) and a melt-in-the-mouth granular danedar grain that cream-boiled ghee simply cannot match.
Pure Desi Ghee has virtually zero moisture (<0.2%), which naturally protects it against microbial spoilage without needing artificial preservatives or refrigeration. Store your GreenFarms 500g jar at ambient room temperature in a dry pantry, away from direct sunlight. Always use a clean, completely dry wooden or stainless steel spoon. It stays pristine for 12+ months.
At comfortable room temperature (20°C–25°C), authentic butter-churned ghee settles into distinct, sugar-like crystalline grains (danedar). On a warm palm, it melts instantly into a golden silk without leaving a waxy, sticky residue. In the refrigerator, it solidifies uniformly without splitting into separate oil or water layers.
